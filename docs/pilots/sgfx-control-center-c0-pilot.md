## Evidence rules

- Use the same timing method for both paths and preserve the unrounded observed values.
- Record interruptions or invalid observations as notes; do not subtract or reconstruct unobserved time.
- Keep the pre-C0 and C0 observations paired by participant role and profile.
- Do not publish a percentage, target, productivity claim, or approval statement from an unpaired or incomplete record.
- External posting and time-entry submission remain manual operator actions.
